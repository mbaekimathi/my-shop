Stock settings

Compulsory inputs

Fields that must be filled before Stock In, Stock Out, or Request Stock can be submitted. Serial-tracked items also require serial numbers that match the quantity.

Stock In

Buying stock into a shop

Open Stock In
  • Shop Which shop receives the stock (per line or shop filter)
  • Quantity Units to add (or serial count for serial items)
  • Buying price Whole-number cost per unit for every stocked line
  • Supplier phone Country dial code + 9-digit national number
  • Supplier name Must match or be selected from live search after phone
  • Payment status Unpaid, paid, or partial
  • Serial numbers Required only when the item tracks serials (count = qty)
  • Buy stock (shop floor) Also requires a verified 6-digit staff ID

Stock Out

Removing stock from a shop

Open Stock Out
  • Shop Which shop the stock leaves from
  • Quantity Units to remove (must not exceed available stock)
  • Reason Waste, transfer, display, or return
  • Refund Yes or no
  • Refund amount Required when refund is yes — whole number greater than zero
  • Serial numbers Required only when the item tracks serials (must exist in that shop)

Request Stock

Asking another shop for stock

Open Request
  • Requesting shop Click a shop column header to mark who is requesting
  • From shop Enter quantity under the shop(s) you are requesting from
  • Quantity At least one line with quantity greater than zero
  • Different shops Requesting shop and from-shop cannot be the same
  • Not required Supplier, payment, reason, refund, or serials at request time
  • Shop floor create May also require a verified 6-digit staff ID

Submit stays blocked until every ready line has these details. The submit panel jumps to the first incomplete field if you try early.
